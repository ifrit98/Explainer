## Probe (model.md)

14 findings, 6 "main". Adopted 8. The important one: the model claimed "anchored expectations make disinflation cheaper", but its own toy shows the opposite for a hike (weakly anchored, inflation falls further for about the same output loss). What the toy supports is the shock result: under anchoring a supply shock fades; under weak anchoring it persists. The model row now says that. Also adopted: the page now says the toy leaves out the forward-looking expectations channel, gives the output gap a meaning in numbers, ties the turn in output to the end of the hike, and states full pass-through as an assumption.
