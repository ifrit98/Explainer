# Narrative: rates-inflation

> Pass 3. The page follows these beats, top to bottom.

## 1. Reader before and after

- **Before:** A technical reader who knows what inflation and an interest rate are and follows news about central banks. No economics training.
- **After:** "A higher policy rate raises the real cost of borrowing, so demand falls and, a year or two later, inflation; how much and how fast are estimates, anchored expectations decide whether shocks fade, and two disputed views say a hike can raise inflation."

## 2. Question and motive

- **Question:** how does raising one interest rate lower inflation, how long does it take, and what is disputed? Answer first, in the lede.
- **Why care:** central bank decisions are in the news every month; the effect arrives long after the decision, and economists disagree about parts of it.
- **Why this approach:** the outcome depends on assumptions; a toy model with a switch for each assumption shows what each one does.
