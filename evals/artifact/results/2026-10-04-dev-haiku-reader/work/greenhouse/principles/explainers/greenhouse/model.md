# Model: how greenhouse gases warm the Earth

**Central question:** Why is the surface warmer than sunlight alone predicts, and why does more CO2 make it warmer?

**Reader:** technical, school physics, new to climate. Knows energy conservation and what a molecule is. Does not know Stefan–Boltzmann, Wien, lapse rate, emission height, forcing.

**Representation:** Stage 1, controlled prose with one text figure. The argument is a sequential causal chain; one vertical profile figure carries the only spatial idea.

## Entities
- Sunlight (short-wave, peak ~0.5 µm). Infrared from Earth (long-wave, peak ~10 µm).
- Greenhouse gases: CO2, H2O, CH4 (3+ atoms, vibrations change dipole). N2, O2: transparent to IR.
- Emission level: the altitude from which infrared escapes to space.
- Lapse rate: temperature fall with height in the troposphere, set by convection.

## Causal chain
1. Absorbed sunlight 238 W/m² must be balanced by 238 W/m² of IR out.
2. A body that emits 238 W/m² has T = 255 K (Stefan–Boltzmann).
3. Surface is 288 K and emits 390 W/m². The atmosphere stops ~150 W/m² of it.
4. Greenhouse gases absorb and emit IR; IR reaches space mainly from the emission level (~5 km).
5. Air cools with height (6.5 K/km), so emission level at 255 K implies surface ≈ 288 K.
6. More CO2 raises the emission level → colder emitter → less IR out → imbalance → column warms until balance returns.

## Confusion points
- "Gases reflect heat" — they absorb and re-emit.
- "CO2 is saturated" — the emission level still rises; band wings widen.
- One-layer model overshoots (303 K).
- Mechanism needs a lapse rate; with an isothermal atmosphere raising the emission level changes nothing (stratosphere case).

## Epistemic status
- Steps 1–5: established physics and observation.
- 5.35 ln(C/C0): fit to radiative-transfer calculations.
- Climate sensitivity 2.5–4 K: estimate (IPCC AR6 likely range).

## Scope
Out: clouds in detail, ocean heat uptake and time lags, carbon cycle, history of measurements.
