## 4. Beats

Each beat answers the question the beat before it raised. Write the bridge: "so…", "but…", "therefore…". Each beat says what it shows, and shows what it says.

Kinds: hook, instance, notice, name, objection, approach, mechanism, generalize, test (predict), close, payoff, recap.

## 5. Concrete to symbol

For each general statement: the instance first, then the binding said aloud ("here n is 5"), then the symbol. Check the symbol against two or three instances.

## 6. Links between representations

Each pair of representations of one quantity (a number and a picture, a formula and a shape), and how the link is made at the same moment: color, words, and a highlight.

## 7. Close the loop

- **Answer:** the opening question, answered in its own words.
- **General argument:** what holds in every case, and why, said aloud.
- **Payoff:** what the result lets the reader do, and one connection. Scope pointers for the next question.
